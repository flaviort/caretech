# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: decision makers at Brazilian healthcare organizations (hospitals, clinics, labs) who own or answer for IT: CIOs, IT managers, administrative and financial directors, and hospital executives who need a partner for IT management, data, or staffing. They arrive comparing consultancies, usually after a pain point (understaffed IT, unreliable infrastructure, data nobody trusts, an ERP migration).

Secondary: leaders at organizations outside healthcare with the same problems. Services are phrased so they fit any sector; healthcare is the lead story, not a gate.

## Product Purpose

CareTech (CareTech IT) is a Brazilian IT, data intelligence, and digital transformation consultancy founded in 2022, built on the founder's 15+ years running IT in critical environments, mostly healthcare. The site must make a visitor understand what CareTech does, trust that it can run critical IT, and start a conversation (WhatsApp, e-mail, or contact form).

Slogan: "Tecnologia que Cuida. Inteligência que Transforma."

## Positioning

Technology with business vision: CareTech acts as a strategic partner that connects technology to the organization's goals, not just a vendor that delivers tech. Its proof is having taken over the full IT management of a large hospital institution, an environment where availability, information security, and process quality are critical.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate on desktop at work and on mobile from WhatsApp links. Brazilian Portuguese only (pt-BR). National coverage ("Atendimento Nacional"), based in Curitiba region (41 area code).

## Product Principles

1. Lead with business outcomes, not tech jargon.
2. Earn trust through the critical-environment story (hospital IT) and calm competence, never hype.
3. Make contact effortless from every page, WhatsApp first for Brazilian buyers.
4. Say only what is true: no invented clients, numbers, or certifications.
